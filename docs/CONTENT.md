# Question correctness and content policy

All bundled stems, options and explanations are original practice material. References point to conceptual supporting sources, not purported PRC question provenance. FAA maintenance references are educational: they do not replace manufacturer-approved repair data or Philippine aviation rules.

No assertion of full coverage: missing advanced areas include substantial compressible/rotary-wing/wind-tunnel problems, indeterminate structural analysis, detailed aircraft design/repair regulations, advanced differential equations, propulsion design and management/ethics cases. Track competency-level TOS mapping when expanding the bank. This first foundation bank is meant for usability and learning-flow validation.

Every record requires four distinct choices, a single integer correct index 0–3, explanation, subject/topic, source title/HTTPS URL/locator, review status, reviewer and review date. Author self-review is labeled `source-checked`; independent instructors use `approved`. Automated checks prove structure and arithmetic fixtures, not universal educational accuracy. Correctness claims require independent item-by-item review.

Faculty checklist: verify current governing source, precise section/chapter, complete assumptions/units, one unambiguously correct option, plausible distractors, explanation and calculation, educational level, absence of copyrighted question copying, and version/reviewer/date. One discovered citation correction during self-review: oath provision is PD 1570 Section 19, not Section 21. This illustrates why source locators are audited separately from schema checks.

AI may draft coursework plans in the private dashboard. It cannot directly write the scored bank or alter keys. User-imported `approved` metadata is a trust decision, not an authenticity certificate. Do not distribute a foundation test candidate as a validated board review course. There is no pass guarantee or calculated probability of passing.
