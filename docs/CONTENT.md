# Question correctness and content policy

All bundled stems, options and explanations are original practice material. References point to conceptual supporting sources, not purported PRC question provenance. FAA maintenance references are educational: they do not replace manufacturer-approved repair data or Philippine aviation rules.

PRC blueprint source, verified 2026-11-13: https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/exam%20program%20nov%202026%20%28aero%29.pdf

No assertion of full coverage: missing advanced areas include substantial compressible/rotary-wing/wind-tunnel problems, indeterminate structural analysis, detailed aircraft design/repair regulations, advanced differential equations, propulsion design and management/ethics cases. Track competency-level TOS mapping when expanding the bank. This foundation bank supports usability and learning-flow validation. Version 2026.11-foundations.2 preserves all 72 v0.1 IDs/keys and adds 288 original items: most are parameterized calculations with explicit assumptions, plus 24 additional construction concepts. There are 60 per subject, allowing 50 distinct selections. This is not a claim of 360 separate competencies.

Every record requires three or three or four distinct choices, a single integer correct index 0–3, explanation, subject/topic, source title/HTTPS URL/locator, review status, reviewer and review date. Author self-review is labeled `source-checked`; independent instructors use `approved`. Automated checks prove structure and arithmetic fixtures, not universal educational accuracy. Correctness claims require independent item-by-item review.

Faculty checklist: verify current governing source, precise section/chapter, complete assumptions/units, one unambiguously correct option, plausible distractors, explanation and calculation, educational level, absence of copyrighted question copying, and version/reviewer/date. One discovered citation correction during self-review: oath provision is PD 1570 Section 19, not Section 21. This illustrates why source locators are audited separately from schema checks.

AI may draft coursework plans in the private dashboard and select up to three allowlisted focus topics using server aggregate practice counts. It cannot directly write the scored bank or alter keys. User-imported `approved` metadata is a trust decision, not an authenticity certificate. Do not distribute a foundation test candidate as a validated board review course. There is no pass guarantee or calculated probability of passing.


## User-supplied private guides

An explicitly user-requested private study copy may retain supplied guide stems,
A-C choices, explanations and printed keys. `source-key-verified` means the PDF
footer agrees with the extracted key, NOT independent instructor approval. It is
allowed only with `distribution: private-study`, `rights: user-provided-private`,
source PDF SHA-256, exact page/question locator, original letter key and key page.
Unapproved AI drafts remain excluded. Ambiguous/figure-dependent records are held
for manual review. Public packaging rejects private guide content. See PRIVATE_GUIDES.md.
